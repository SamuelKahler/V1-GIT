# Sports Edge V29 — Sport Home + Restored Boards

Stable base: V28 / V24 render lineage.

## What changed
- Added a new sport-first homepage with MLB, NHL, NFL, NBA, and NCAAB sport cards.
- Added dedicated sport landing pages so future data does not get crammed into one space.
- MLB has an active command center that routes to Today’s Picks, Series Board, Trend Database, Performance Lab, Research Journal, and Model Center.
- Research Journal content remains restored and visible from both nav and MLB workspace.
- Series Board content remains restored and visible as its own page.
- Performance Lab is reorganized into:
  - quick summary
  - category strength board
  - daily bettor checklist
  - searchable actual bet log
- Preserved V28 pick/details behavior and did not change the working MLB render pipeline.

## Design direction
The app now starts with sport selection first. Each sport gets its own color identity and room for future data.

## Important
NHL, NFL, NBA, and NCAAB are placeholder workspaces until sport-specific data is added.
