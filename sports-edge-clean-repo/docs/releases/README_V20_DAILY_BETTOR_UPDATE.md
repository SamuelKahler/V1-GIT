# Sports Edge V20 - Daily Bettor Dashboard

## Product Mindset
V20 changes the app from a research database first into a daily bettor dashboard first.

The first question the app answers is now:

> What should I look at today?

## Key Updates
- Homepage rebuilt around today's slate.
- Official Plays are separated from Research Plays.
- Official Plays require a unit size attached.
- Research Plays are plays found by the model/data but not staked yet.
- Plays are grouped by bettor-friendly categories:
  - First Five
  - Moneyline
  - Over
  - Under
  - Series
- Today's Picks page now shows:
  - Official Plays
  - Research Plays
  - Historical Results Archive
- Series Board remains clean and card-based from V18/V19.
- Verified results and truth-first grading remain from V19.

## Important Rule
If a pick does not have a unit size, it is not treated as an official bet. It stays as a Research Play until units are attached.

## Why
Consumers should not have to understand all the raw trend data first. They should see the best daily opportunities immediately, then click deeper if they want the supporting logic.
